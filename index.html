DOWNTOWN OWEN SOUND

Frog Ponds Cafe

The cozy downtown café regulars call the best coffee in town — 4.6 stars across 104 Google reviews.

209 8th St E, Owen Sound, ON N4K 1L2 · (519) 371-7000

Sips & bites

  • Lattes and cold brews — try the caramel latte or a flavoured americano
  • Lavender hot chocolate and white hot chocolate
  • Loose leaf teas, smoothies and freshly squeezed grapefruit juice
  • Fresh baking: scones (apple brie is the fancy one), cranberry orange loaf, chocolate chip cookies and muffins
  • Breakfast sandwiches, oatmeal, and the naan flatbread with chicken, roasted red pepper and goat cheese

Vegan-friendly options, takeout, and a bright, peaceful room to land in.

Hours

Monday to Saturday: 7:00 AM – 4:00 PM · Sunday: Closed
